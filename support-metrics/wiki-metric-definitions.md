# Support metrics: how each one is measured

Status: approved by Shanise, 2026-10-07. Goals and current baselines stay on the Success metrics page. This page only says how each number is calculated.

## Rules that apply to every metric

**Tracked ticket.** A Support conversation that has at least one reply from a human teammate. Tickets handled only by Fin, or resolved by Fin, are not counted anywhere. A ticket Fin escalated and a teammate then answered is counted.

**Business hours.** Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 6:00pm Eastern Time (9 business hours a day). The clock does not run on weekends, outside those hours, or on these holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Fourth of July
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

A ticket that arrives outside business hours starts its clock at the next opening time.

**Week.** Monday to Sunday.

## The metrics

| Metric | How it is measured |
|---|---|
| **Open tickets** | Tickets open right now, snoozed tickets excluded. It includes every assignee: any teammate, and unassigned tickets. It counts tickets Fin is not still handling alone. It is recorded as an end-of-day count each day. |
| **First response** | Business hours from ticket creation to the first human teammate reply. The SLA is met at 1 business day or less (9 business hours). The median is reported against the 4-hour goal. |
| **Tickets closed per week** | Each tracked ticket is counted once, in the week of its latest close. Team total includes all tickets. For individuals, a ticket is credited to its assignee. If it is unassigned, it is credited to the teammate who closed it, unless it is spam, an out-of-office reply or another automated message (those are not credited to anyone). |
| **Reopen rate** | Share of tickets closed in the period that have one or more reopens, using Intercom's raw reopen count. A customer reply to a closed ticket (such as a thank-you) counts as a reopen. This is kept for now and can be tightened later. |
| **Time to resolution** | Business hours from ticket creation to the ticket's latest close. The median is reported against the 12-hour goal, with the 90th percentile shown separately for the long tail. |
| **CSAT** | Stage 1: response rate, which is tickets with a rating divided by tickets sent a survey. Stage 2: the share of ratings that are 4 or 5, shown with the number of ratings. Per-teammate CSAT is hidden until there are enough ratings to be meaningful. |
