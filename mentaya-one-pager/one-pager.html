Getting reimbursed for therapy with Mentaya

Mentaya helps you get money back from your insurance for sessions with a therapist who is out-of-network, without the paperwork.

How it works

  1. Create a free Mentaya account. Your therapist will send you an invite link. Mentaya can also check whether your plan has out-of-network benefits.
  2. Attend your sessions as usual. You pay your therapist their full fee, as you would for any out-of-network care.
  3. Mentaya submits your claims. After each session your therapist confirms it happened and Mentaya automatically files the claim with your insurance company. There is nothing for you to submit.
  4. Mentaya follows up. If your insurer needs corrections or denies a claim, Mentaya fixes and resubmits it at no extra cost.
  5. Your insurer pays you directly. Reimbursement comes from your insurance company by mailed check (or direct deposit, if you set that up with them), along with an Explanation of Benefits.

What it costs

Mentaya charges a 5% processing fee per claim (for a $200 session, that's $10). Therapists use Mentaya for free. If Mentaya is unable to successfully submit a claim, you get your fee back.

Good to know

  • Your plan needs out-of-network benefits. These are usually PPO, POS, and some high-deductible plans. HMOs, Medicaid, and traditional Medicare generally don't have them.
  • Deductible first. Most plans reimburse only after you've paid your out-of-network deductible. Claims submitted before then are applied toward it, and that's why Mentaya submits every session.
  • After your deductible, your plan reimburses a portion of its "allowed amount." Your copay or coinsurance is the part it does not cover. On average, Mentaya clients get back about 62% of therapy costs.
  • Timing varies. Insurers can take anywhere from about a week to 45 days to process a claim.
Why not just submit superbills yourself? You can, but claims are often rejected over missing information or incorrect codes, and every plan has a filing deadline. Mentaya handles submissions and follow-up so you don't miss out.
